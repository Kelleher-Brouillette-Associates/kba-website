Our approach

Practical by design, tailored by default.

We know restaurant owners aren’t looking for a long consulting engagement. We keep the process light and the disruption minimal, and we give you something you can act on.

We protect your identity. We won’t recommend changes to your menu, staff or culture unless you ask us to. Our job is to help more people discover and enjoy what you already do well.

What we review

Six areas. One clear plan.

Our comprehensive assessment covers up to six areas. Each one is included or left out based on what you need, and it all comes together in one phased, costed report.

01

Site Evaluation

The foundation of every engagement. We visit, walk through and learn your restaurant from you.

  • Owner conversation about background, operations, hours and goals
  • Staff conversations about what they see and their ideas for improvement
  • Storefront review: signage, curb appeal, visibility and sidewalk presence
  • Front of house: lighting, atmosphere, layout, seating and menu readability
  • Back of house: kitchen organization, storage and general workflow
  • Quick wins: improvements that are fast and inexpensive to make
02

Digital Presence Evaluation

Many independent restaurants are hard to find, or show up inconsistently, online. We find the gaps and recommend fixes.

  • Consistency check across your website, social media, Google Maps and Apple Maps
  • Review & ordering sites like Yelp, TripAdvisor, DoorDash, Grubhub and Uber Eats
  • Reservation platforms like OpenTable and Resy
  • Website evaluation: searchability, mobile usability, design and accuracy
  • Online menu review for accuracy, formatting and readability
  • Website mock-up before any committed build
03

Online Reputation Evaluation

Reviews tell a story. We read it, and then turn what customers are saying into fixes.

  • Ratings & reviews across Google, Yelp, TripAdvisor and more
  • Recurring themes in service, consistency, atmosphere and wait times
  • How you respond today, or whether you respond at all
  • Review response strategy: tone, timing, and what to say and avoid
  • Operational fixes based on what reviews reveal
04

Competitive Positioning Evaluation

How you compare to the restaurants around you, and how to stand out before a customer walks through the door.

  • Competitor mapping of direct and indirect competitors nearby
  • Differentiation: signage, street presence and menu positioning
  • Storefront improvements with estimated costs
  • Menu analysis for readability, organization and pricing in context
  • Small, high-impact investments that change how customers see you
05

Cost & Spend Evaluation

For owners who are willing to share purchasing data, a high-level look at standout expenses worth a closer review.

  • Supplier invoices to look for over-ordering, waste and alternative sourcing
  • Menu item profitability: which dishes earn money and which cost money
  • Supplier review: competitiveness and whether to consolidate
  • Service contracts and recurring subscriptions
  • Strictly confidential. Your data is used only for your benefit
06

Technology & Systems Evaluation

Practical, cost-conscious advice. We never recommend technology for its own sake.

  • Point of sale: a good fit for your volume, menu and reporting needs?
  • Online ordering: delivery platform fees and alternatives
  • Reservations: whether a tool makes sense for your traffic
  • Inventory & waste tracking: whether it would pay for itself
  • Payroll & scheduling software review
How it works

Seven simple steps. Zero surprises.

Every engagement starts with a site visit. The scope is agreed up front, priced as a fixed fee, and you’re never obligated to go beyond the assessment itself.

Schedule step one
  1. 01
    Week 0

    Initial conversation Free

    A short conversation, ideally in person. We want to understand what’s working, what isn’t and what you’d most like to improve. If it’s clear we can add value, we’ll outline a proposed scope and fee.

  2. 02
    Week 0

    Scope agreement

    We agree on which services are included and confirm a fixed fee. We keep the paperwork to a minimum.

  3. 03
    Week 1

    On-site evaluation

    We spend two to three hours at your restaurant, including the back of house, and talk with you and your staff. We stay out of the way of your service.

  4. 04
    Weeks 1–5

    Research & analysis

    We audit your digital presence, analyze your reviews, research your competitors and, if it’s in scope, review your spending.

  5. 05
    Week 6

    Assessment report

    We deliver your written report, organized into phases with cost estimates and expected impact, and walk you through it in person.

  6. 06
    Weeks 7–11

    Follow-on implementation

    If you’d like help with the digital work, like a website build or a profile setup, we scope and price it separately. It’s entirely optional.

  7. 07
    Month to month

    Continued maintenance

    Ongoing hosting, website updates and platform upkeep for a simple monthly fee, if and when you want it.

The deliverable

Inside your assessment report.

The report is the main product of every engagement. It’s a written document, a slide deck or both, and it lays out our findings and a clear path forward.

  • Executive summary

    The most important findings and the recommendations we feel most strongly about, for when you want the headline first.

  • Phased recommendations

    Sorted into now, next and later, so you can act without feeling overwhelmed.

  • Cost estimates

    Based on typical local vendor and contractor pricing, and clearly labeled as estimates rather than quotes.

  • Expected outcomes

    What each change should do for you, such as more foot traffic, better reviews or lower food costs.

Three phases

Start today. Plan for tomorrow.
