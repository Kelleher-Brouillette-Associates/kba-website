Our story

Three generations, one oven.

Marigold started with a hand-written recipe card and a secondhand oven. Today it’s still family-run: the same 48-hour dough, the same sauce, and a lot more neighbors around the tables.

We source tomatoes from Italy, cheese from Vermont, and greens from farms within an hour of the restaurant.
