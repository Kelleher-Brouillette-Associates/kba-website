The assessment report

Recommendations you can actually act on.

Every recommendation comes with a cost estimate and an expected outcome. Each one is sorted into a phase, so you can start right away without feeling overwhelmed.

1
Phase one · Now

Low cost, high impact

Immediate actions you can take this month, usually with little or no spend.

2
Phase two · Next

Modest investment

Near-term actions that need a decision or a small budget, with costs spelled out.

3
Phase three · Later

Longer-term upside

Higher-commitment moves with longer payback, and potentially significant returns.
